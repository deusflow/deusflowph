# Hogwarts Art Direction & Library Ritual Guide

## 1. Core Philosophy: Ritual, Not Fireworks
- **Atmosphere First**: The setting is night in the Hogwarts library — dark aged wood, candlelight bokeh, dust in sun/moonbeams.
- **Singularity of Focus**: All magic manifests in a single focal point (Center-Top of the screen). The surrounding environment remains serene and static.
- **Ritual Pacing**:
  1. **Stillness (0.4s)**: Shards hover gently before flight, as if awaiting the incantation.
  2. **Spiral Wave Flight (1.2–1.8s)**: Shards fly in a staggered, gentle spiral curve.
  3. **Manifestation Landing**: Wet ink soaking into parchment — a soft saturation pulse fading over 0.6s, NOT a blinding explosion.
  4. **Resting Breathing**: Assembled shapes breathe subtly with a soft gold edge-glow.

---

## 2. Palette: Book Matter, Not Cosmic Gold
- **Parchment (50%)**: Warm creamy beige (`#e8dcc0` / `RGB(0.93, 0.88, 0.78)`).
- **Archival Ink (35%)**: Deep blue-black (`#1a1f2e` / `RGB(0.14, 0.16, 0.24)`).
- **Enchanted Gold Accent (15%)**: Fine luminous edge-glow (`#fce2b8` / `#d8b888`). Gold is the magical trail, not the whole matter.
- **Background**: Dark oak `#0d0a08` gradient to warm amber `#1c130c`. NO pure black `#000000`.

---

## 3. Anti-"Cheap" Checklist (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ Millions of disconnected space particles.
- ❌ Spinning wireframe toruses, astral rings, or standalone orbits.
- ❌ Blinding bloom fireworks or explosion flashes upon landing.
- ❌ Pure `#000000` void backgrounds.
- ❌ Multiple unrelated visual effects competing for viewer attention.
