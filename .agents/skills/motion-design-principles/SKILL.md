# Motion Design Principles: The Ritual of Movement

## 1. Pacing & Staging
- **Anticipation (0.4s Stillness)**: Never start moving immediately upon scroll. A momentary hover gives gravity to the action.
- **Wave Arrival (1.2–1.8s Duration)**: Stagger particle delays based on spatial distance and random noise seeds so the form arrives like an organic wave rather than a rigid block.
- **Settling Pulse (0.6s Decay)**: The transition ends not with an abrupt stop or explosion, but with a subtle saturation/brightness pulse (ink settling into the fibers of old paper).

## 2. Velocity-Driven Turbulence
- Fast gestures cause forms to disintegrate into wind/dust.
- Halting restores crystalline order immediately through magnetic dampening.
